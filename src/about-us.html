About

Most people think strategy starts with answers. I’ve learned it starts with better questions.

My work sits at the intersection of clarity, storytelling, and execution, helping ideas grow without losing their soul.

Ash’s story

  1. Shot 1Wide

    I’ve spent most of my career inside creative work, directing stories, building brands, and sitting in rooms where decisions mattered more than they looked.

  2. Shot 2Push in

    Over time, I noticed the same pattern repeating, the best work didn’t come from certainty or speed, it came from people who were willing to slow down and ask better questions.

  3. Shot 3Close-up

    That’s the space I care about now, where clarity replaces noise and conversation becomes a tool for real progress.

  4. Shot 4Two-shot

    Today, I work as a creative strategist and host of Amplified, a long-form podcast built around conversations that carry weight. It’s where I explore ideas in public, with people who are building meaningful work and living intentionally, long before the world notices.
