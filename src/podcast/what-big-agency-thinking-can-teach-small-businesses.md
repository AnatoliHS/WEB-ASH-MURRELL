In this episode, I sit down with Graham Copestake, former Account Director at Leo Burnett, to talk about what years inside the advertising world can teach us about creativity, communication, and building better businesses. Graham shares stories from his career in agency life, the lessons he's carried into working with small businesses and students, and why strong ideas still need real purpose behind them.
