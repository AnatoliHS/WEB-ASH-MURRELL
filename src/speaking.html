Speaking

Talks that help people slow down, think clearly, and do work that actually matters.

A speaker who helps people slow down, think clearly, and ask better questions about their work and lives.

Guest Speaker Request

I don’t hand out frameworks, keynotes or five-step plans.

I help teams and leaders slow down long enough to think clearly about what actually matters.

This isn’t a presentation.

It’s a working conversation. I show up, listen to the room, and ask the questions that haven’t surfaced yet, the ones that shift how people see the problem, the work, or what they’re really trying to do.

I follow the energy.

I adapt in real time. I don’t fill space with noise. Every question, every pause, every redirect is intentional.

People don’t leave with inspiration quotes.

They leave with clarity. Or at least better questions. A stronger sense of what matters, and what to do next.

I work best with teams stuck in the weeds.

Leaders who need to think out loud with someone who won’t just nod along. Organizations that value depth over performance.

If you’re looking for someone to “inspire” your team with platitudes, I’m not your person. But if you want someone to help them think better,

let’s talk.
