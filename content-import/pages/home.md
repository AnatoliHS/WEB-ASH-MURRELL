#### About

## I’m a brand strategist and the host of [**Amplified**](/podcast), a long-form podcast built around conversations that carry weight.

## My work sits at the intersection of clarity, storytelling, and intentional living… I love helping people slow down, think more honestly, and build work that lasts.

## AMP Visual Media is where I apply this thinking in practice, partnering with brands who care about substance over noise.

Based in Canada

Working with creatives and brands everywhere.
