#### About

# Most people think strategy starts with answers. I’ve learned it starts with better questions. My work sits at the intersection of clarity, storytelling, and execution, helping ideas grow without losing their soul.

![Image of the company's staff members in conference room](../media/M0S3EWTmoVS5ow8P9pdBGvJaGmM.jpg)

The Podcast

## I’ve spent most of my career inside creative work, directing stories, building brands, and sitting in rooms where decisions mattered more than they looked.

## Over time, I noticed the same pattern repeating, the best work didn’t come from certainty or speed, it came from people who were willing to slow down and ask better questions.

## That’s the space I care about now, where clarity replaces noise and conversation becomes a tool for real progress. Today, I work as a creative strategist and host of **Amplified**, a long-form podcast built around conversations that carry weight. It’s where I explore ideas in public, with people who are building meaningful work and living intentionally, long before the world notices.
