Eric Johnston grew up in a world where performance wasn't some far-off dream. It was right there in the house, in the stories, in the chaos, and in the shadow of his dad, Dan 'Bullwhip' Johnston. What I loved about this conversation is how quickly it moved past comedy and into the real stuff underneath it. Family legacy. Ambition. Privacy. Parenting. The strange pressure of being known by people who don't actually know you.
